# Deutsch Journey — Menschen A1.1 Learning Strategy

## Purpose

This document defines how **Deutsch Journey** should guide a learner through **Menschen A1.1** and turn each lesson into practical German.

The app should not stop at memorizing vocabulary. Every lesson should follow this loop:

```text
SEE → UNDERSTAND → HEAR → SAY → USE → RECALL → REUSE
```

The lesson themes and pronunciation progression are aligned with Menschen A1.1. The dialogues, exercises, examples, and life missions below are original practice material for the app.

---

# 1. Core Learning Strategy

For every lesson, the learner should answer:

1. What words do I need?
2. What sentence patterns do I need?
3. What grammar helps me build them?
4. What should the German sound like?
5. Where would I use it in real life?
6. Can I use it without looking at the book?

Every lesson therefore contains:

- vocabulary
- sentence patterns
- grammar in context
- pronunciation
- listening
- speaking
- reading/writing
- mini-dialogues
- Germany scenarios
- spaced repetition
- real-life missions

---

# 2. The Learning Loop for Every New Word

Example:

```text
der Bahnhof
train station
محطة القطار
```

## Step 1 — See

```text
der Bahnhof
Plural: die Bahnhöfe
```

Always learn nouns with the article.

## Step 2 — Connect

Attach an image and situation:

```text
🚉 train station
platform
ticket
Berlin Hauptbahnhof
```

## Step 3 — Hear

Play:

```text
der Bahnhof
```

Then:

```text
Wo ist der Bahnhof?
```

## Step 4 — Repeat

Learner records the word and sentence.

## Step 5 — Build

```text
ist / Wo / Bahnhof / der
```

Learner builds:

```text
Wo ist der Bahnhof?
```

## Step 6 — Use

Germany Mode:

```text
You arrive in a new city and need the train station.
Ask someone for help.
```

Target:

```text
Entschuldigung, wo ist der Bahnhof?
```

## Step 7 — Review

The word returns later through:

- flashcards
- listening
- article games
- sentence building
- speaking
- AI chat
- real-life situations

# 3. Daily Study Routine

A normal session should take around **25–35 minutes**.

## Warm-up — 5 minutes

Review:

- 5 old words
- 3 old sentences
- 1 listening sentence

## Menschen Study — 10–15 minutes

Study a small section of the book.

For every useful word:

```text
Add word
↓
Add article/plural if needed
↓
Listen
↓
Create one useful sentence
```

Target around **8–12 important words** per session.

## Active Practice — 10 minutes

Complete:

```text
3 sentence-building exercises
5 flashcards
2 listening exercises
2 speaking attempts
```

## Life Mission — 5 minutes

Use the lesson in a realistic situation.

Examples:

- introduce yourself
- describe family
- ask a price
- arrange a meeting
- order food
- ask for transport
- explain what you did today



- listen to a statement and a question
- choose which one sounds like a question
- shadow the complete phrase

# 5. Listening Strategy

Listening should increase gradually.

## Level 1 — Word

```text
Bahnhof
```

Choose the correct image.

## Level 2 — Phrase

Audio:

```text
der Bahnhof
```

Type the word.

## Level 3 — Sentence

Audio:

```text
Wo ist der Bahnhof?
```

Choose the meaning.

## Level 4 — Dictation

Audio only. Learner types the full sentence.

## Level 5 — Conversation

```text
A: Entschuldigung, wo ist der Bahnhof?
B: Dort links.
```

Ask comprehension questions.

## Level 6 — Real-Life Environment

Later add mild background sound:

- station
- café
- street
- supermarket

Only add noise after the learner understands clean audio.

# 6. Speaking Strategy

Speaking should use four stages.

## 1 — Listen

```text
Ich komme aus Ägypten.
```

## 2 — Repeat With Text

Text stays visible.

## 3 — Repeat Without Text

Only the meaning/image remains.

## 4 — Use It in Conversation

AI:

```text
Woher kommst du?
```

Learner must produce the answer without seeing it.

This final step proves active learning.

# 8. AI Chat Strategy

Use three chat levels.

```text

```

Best for a new lesson.

```text

```

## Free Chat

```text

```

The AI should stay inside vocabulary and grammar already learned.

# 9. Vocabulary Mastery Strategy

A word should move through:

```text

↓

↓
RECALLED
↓
USED IN A SENTENCE
↓
UNDERSTOOD IN AUDIO
↓
USED IN SPEECH
↓
MASTERED
```

Example:

```text

✓ translation recognized
✓ article remembered
✓ sentence built
✓ audio understood
✓ used in Germany Mode
✓ recalled after several days
```

Only then should the app treat it as mastered.

Use:

```text
EXAMPLE → PATTERN → SMALL RULE → EXERCISE → SPEAKING
```

Example:

```text
Ich habe einen Computer.
Ich brauche einen Computer.
```

Then show:

```text
der Computer → einen Computer
```

Then exercise:

```text
Ich brauche ___ Laptop.

A. ein
B. einen
C. eine
```

Finally speaking:

```text
Was brauchst du für deine Arbeit?
```

# 16. Mistake-Based Learning

Every repeated mistake should create future practice.

Example:

```text
wrong: die Bahnhof
correct: der Bahnhof
```

Store:

```text
mistake_type: article
word: Bahnhof
expected: der Bahnhof
```

Future exercises can include:

```text
___ Bahnhof
Wo ist ___ Bahnhof?
```

and listening for:

```text
der Bahnhof
```

# 20. Final A1.1 Outcome

By the end of this journey, the learner should be able to produce simple German like:

```text
Hallo! Ich heiße Nabil.
Ich komme aus Ägypten und wohne in Alexandria.
Ich bin Softwareentwickler.
Ich spreche Arabisch und Englisch und ein bisschen Deutsch.
Das ist meine Familie.
Ich fotografiere gern.
Hast du am Samstag Zeit?
Treffen wir uns um sieben?
Ich möchte einen Kaffee, bitte.
Entschuldigung, wo ist die U-Bahn?
Ich steige am Hauptbahnhof aus.
Heute habe ich gearbeitet und Deutsch gelernt.
Im Sommer bin ich nach Deutschland gefahren.
```

The real goal is not just:

```text
I recognize A1.1 German.
```

It is:

```text
I can retrieve A1.1 German and use it in real life.
```

# 21. Most Important Product Rule

Never allow learning to end here:

```text
Bahnhof = train station
```

Always continue:

```text
der Bahnhof
↓
Wo ist der Bahnhof?
↓
hear it
↓
say it
↓
use it in chat
↓
use it in a Germany situation
↓
recall it days later
```

That loop should be the foundation of **Deutsch Journey**.
